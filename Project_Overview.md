# Critter Creamery

create a website for a fake ice cream business - "critter creamery".  it's an ice cream shop that has different types of bugs in all of their flavors.

the website should be colorful and fun.

# Front Page

at the top of the fold show a few images of people enjoying bug ice cream.  close up realistic photos of people eating ice cream with bugs in it.  kids or adults with ice cream and bug parts dripping down their chins.

below that list the top ten favorite flavors, and include high rez zoomed in photos of each.  each ice cream should be packed with bugs.

each image should have a clear but not distracting title with the business name.

# Swarm It

in addition to the flavors talk about the option to "swarm it" for each order, which doubles the amount of bugs.  include an image here of a smiling child with an ice cream cone and bugs swarming all around him.

# flavors

on a separate page show all of their different flavors.  use different categories including favorites, seasonal, exotic, and more.  include at least fifty different flavors here.  do not show an image for each flavor, but have an image for each section.

for all bug flavors use a variety of different bugs, including gross ones like centipedes, worms, roaches, Jerusalem crickets, and more. some flavors should include multiple types of bugs.

# About Us

include a bizarre origin story in the "about" page talking about how the founder (make up a name) accidentally ate ice cream with some bug in it and got hooked. 

# testimonials

include a testimonials page with people commenting on their favorite flavors.

# our bugs

include a page that talks about the humane and ethical way in which their  ugs are.produced and raised.  should be over the top and include things like story time at night, free range bugs, and more.

include a few images here of farmers holding bugs, caring for them, sitting down smiling with bugs all over them.

# build your own sunday

include a page that lets people build their own "bug Sunday", which is a builder that lets them choose the flavor (from the list), different bugs for toppings (dried meal worm, grasshopper legs, chocolate covered ladybugs, etc) and other insects related Sunday items (and some normal ones).
